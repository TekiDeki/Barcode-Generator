Barcode.Generator

Barcodes that print clean and never phone home

Type or paste your codes, pick the symbology, and take vector SVG, a sharp PNG, or a full sheet of labels. Everything is drawn in this tab, so nothing is uploaded and the page keeps working with the internet off.

Symbology

13 digits. The last one is the check digit, and it is filled in for you when you type 12.

Sheet of labels

A4 page, as many labels per row as fit. The bars scale to the label.

Print at 100% scale, with "fit to page" switched off, or the bars will not measure right.

Why this one is different

Nothing leaves the tab

The bars are computed and drawn by this page. No server sees your product codes, and the tool keeps working with the network off.

Vector first

SVG for a printer, PNG at 300 or 600 dpi for a screen. Both keep the module width you asked for, so a scanner reads them at the size you print.

Sheets, not one at a time

Paste a list and print a page of labels with the numbers under each code. Retail units, cartons, shelves and assets, all on one sheet.
